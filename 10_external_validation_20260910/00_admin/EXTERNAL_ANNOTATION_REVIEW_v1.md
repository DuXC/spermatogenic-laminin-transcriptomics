# Review of the external published cell labels

Recorded on 2026-09-10 after inspecting independent-marker donor summaries and before fitting external single-cell ECM contrasts.

All six raw matrices map completely to the 28,666 author-curated cells. The author PMC state consistently expresses MYH11 and TAGLN, with CNN1/DES support and very low PTPRC/SOX9. This supports a contractile mural/peritubular interpretation and its prespecified within-state comparison. The separate perivascular state has much stronger RGS5 expression in the three Crypto donors and is absent from the three reference donors; it cannot enter a donor-matched disease comparison.

The author's fibrotic peritubular state has weaker contractile-marker expression in both groups. Retain its source label and the prespecified broad, author-defined peritubular compartment, but interpret the broad analysis as a mixed stromal compartment. It does not establish a uniform cell identity or histologically proven fibrosis. The two source states are not silently relabelled as one homogeneous cell type. The original PMC-state sensitivity supplies the more specific test of expression within a consistent contractile state; its prespecified sensitivity role is retained.

The 30-cells-in-every-donor rule is unchanged. Neither overall cell counts nor statistical significance can rescue the absent/underrepresented Sertoli, Leydig, endothelial, macrophage or perivascular compartments. All coverage results will be exported. The author's fibrotic state alone also fails the original per-donor threshold because N1 has 25 cells.

Independent-marker review excluded genes in the fixed ECM union, including ACTA2 if present in that union. Source labels, marker values and their donor coverage remain available for audit. Mitochondrial fractions are calculated from original count features; they are compared with the supplied metadata and used only in the fixed mitochondrial sensitivity.
