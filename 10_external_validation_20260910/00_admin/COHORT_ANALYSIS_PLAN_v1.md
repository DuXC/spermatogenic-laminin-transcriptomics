# Cohort analysis plan for the independent extension

Fixed on 2026-09-10 after metadata, assay-scale and published cell-label inspection, before new ECM scores, gene contrasts or enrichment results. This is a prospective local analysis record; the original v3.1 analyses are retained.

## Bulk studies

GSE9210 is the main independent tissue-level validation: all 47 originally labelled NOA biopsies versus all 11 OA biopsies. Each GEO sample is a distinct patient; the original publication explicitly describes a standard common-reference design. The Cy3 pool of 39 donors is an assay reference, not a comparison group or 39 independent observations. GEO defines VALUE as the lowess-normalized natural logarithm of Cy5/Cy3. Its identity with ln(CH1_NORM_INT/CH2_NORM_INT) was confirmed in a blinded scale check. Divide by ln(2) to express gene differences as log2 ratios. No additional logarithm or quantile normalization is applied. Exclude nonbiological control probes and ambiguous/conflicting HGNC assignments. Primary module scores and competitive enrichment use probes observed in all 58 patients and nonconstant mapped gene summaries. Report coverage explicitly. As a missingness sensitivity, gene-level limma fits may use probes observed in at least 80% of each group, with no imputation; this does not replace the complete-member module score or enrichment.

GSE108886 is a second, small public cohort: three individual OA biopsies (GSM2915449, GSM2915451, GSM2915455) versus eight individual NOA biopsies (GSM2915445–GSM2915448, GSM2915450, GSM2915452–GSM2915454). GSM2915444 is a pooled commercial control and is excluded before modeling. The GEO series reports 11 patients and one pooled control. Retain original premeiotic/meiotic diagnoses in metadata without changing the primary NOA-versus-OA contrast. Analyze the deposited quantile-normalized values directly, with no additional transformation. Their compact 6.50–14.44 range is consistent with a transformed signal, but the deposit does not explicitly document the logarithm base; call gene effects differences in deposited normalized-signal units, not verified log2 fold changes. Exclude ambiguous/conflicting mappings and features lacking biological gene assignments; require detection P < 0.01 in at least half of either original clinical group. A sensitivity retains all uniquely mapped, complete, nonconstant probes irrespective of detection P. No inferred ages or clinical measurements are added.

GSE45887 explicitly reanalyzes GSE45885 samples and is not another independent cohort. GSE45885 has commercial RNA references, including one labelled with an age range of 14–64 rather than a single verified adult donor. Its original abstract mentions 18 NOA men and 31 biopsy samples, whereas GEO lists 27 patient arrays. The discovery/validation and subject mapping must be resolved before treating these 27 arrays as independent observations. Both studies remain in the audit table; no formal patient-level external ECM inference is performed from them in this release.

For eligible bulk studies, average multiple uniquely mapped probes with equal weight. Use the frozen six ECM programs and five exploratory marker sets. Gene-standardize across the retained patients in each cohort and calculate equal-weight member averages using a fixed complete gene set within that cohort. Fit unadjusted OLS scores with t-based 95% confidence intervals and two-sided P values; adjust the six ECM tests jointly within cohort and additionally show the family across both new cohorts. Laminin is the focal pre-existing endpoint, not a newly selected result. For all genes, use limma with robust, trend empirical Bayes. Use camera with estimated, nonnegative inter-gene correlation as the primary competitive analysis and fixed 0.01 correlation as a sensitivity. Report all measurable laminin genes, missing members, gene directions relative to the frozen bulk cohorts, postmeiotic-marker adjustment, and every leave-one-patient-out score estimate. The leave-one-out analysis keeps the original full-cohort score standardization and does not remove any sample from the primary model. Do not pool differently defined phenotypes into one meta-analytic effect.

## GSE153947 single-cell extension

The six adult donors are N1/N2/N3 (OA with normal spermatogenesis, ages 31/33/55) and Cr1/Cr2/Cr3 (cryptozoospermia, ages 39/25/36). Their 10x v2 preparation and sequencing workflow are shared. The source paper and GEO agree on patient identities. This is an independent etiology extension, not an iNOA replication cohort. Keep its published clinical measurements as source metadata; no prediction model or clinical association test is fitted to six patients.

Use the six original Alevin gene-count matrices and author-provided metadata for 28,666 curated cells. Counts contain fractional abundance estimates; retain their values without rounding. Map each raw barcode to the original donor-specific cell index. Use frozen HGNC approved symbols and unique aliases, summing rows that resolve to one symbol. Retain every published cell in the primary analysis and calculate original-feature QC for audit. A mitochondrial fraction <0.20 sensitivity uses the same labels and is assessed with the same coverage gate. No integrated or sctransform values enter expression inference.

Before ECM inference, audit author labels with independent lineage markers outside the fixed ECM union: ACTA2/MYH11/TAGLN/CNN1/DES for contractile mural cells; RGS5/CSPG4/MCAM/NOTCH3 for perivascular cells; SOX9/WT1/FSHR/CLDN11/GATA4 for Sertoli cells; STAR/CYP11A1/CYP17A1/HSD3B2/INSL3 for Leydig cells; PECAM1/VWF/EMCN/KDR for endothelial cells; PTPRC/LST1/TYROBP for immune cells. Keep the original annotations and the harmonized names together. The primary broad peritubular compartment combines the author's PMCs and fibrotic peritubular myoid cells. The author's PMC state alone is a prespecified sensitivity that tests dependence on the mixture of these states. Preserve the separate perivascular compartment. Neither a label nor an expression difference is evidence of histologically verified fibrosis in this reanalysis.

Only a somatic compartment with at least 30 annotated cells in every donor can enter 3-versus-3 pseudobulk analysis. Assess peritubular, Sertoli, Leydig, endothelial, macrophage and perivascular compartments. The original labels already show that several compartments fail coverage; no cell threshold is relaxed. Require independent-marker review before accepting a compartment identity. Uncertain compartment identities retain a qualified descriptive interpretation.

Aggregate original counts per donor/compartment. Retain genes with CPM >= 1 in at least three donors, normalize with TMM and fit limma-voom with robust empirical Bayes for Crypto minus OA. Report all gene estimates, 95% intervals and BH adjustment within compartment. Test all six frozen programs with correlation-estimated camera; adjust the family jointly across eligible primary compartments. Fixed-correlation camera, the author PMC-state subset and mitochondrial sensitivity are separate prespecified sensitivity families. Include an age-adjusted sensitivity only when the design is full rank, recording its small residual degrees of freedom. Display donor-level state fractions as captured-cell composition; they are not histological tissue fractions. Gene detection summaries describe positivity of estimated Alevin counts, not proof of protein expression.

## Composition and manuscript claims

Public reference deconvolution is not fitted unless independent reference coverage and held-out mixture validation pass. Here, the adult reference has sparse or absent somatic lineages in several donors; marker adjustment and the within-peritubular-state sensitivity address selected composition concerns without producing purported tissue fractions. Every positive, negative and unstable result is retained. New external evidence may strengthen or narrow the manuscript, and cannot establish causation or clinical predictive value by itself.
