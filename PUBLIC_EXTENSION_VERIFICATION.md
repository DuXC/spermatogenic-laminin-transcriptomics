The extension was rerun on 10 September 2026 in an isolated copy of the proposed source release, using exact copies of the previously released expression matrices, fixed memberships and metadata.

The original PRIDE QUANT.xlsx and publisher Table S5 were re-read to reconstruct the participant-level protein inputs. All 105 supplementary protein groups and 3150 LFQ measurements reconciled.
